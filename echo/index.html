When a change may not be real

  • EF has inter-reader variability of about 5–10 points; a change under 10 points is often noise.
  • AF or other irregular rhythms make EF and Doppler values beat-dependent; tachycardia lowers EF and distorts diastolic indices.
  • Loading conditions (volume overload, dehydration, sepsis, high BP) change filling indices, RVSP and IVC.
  • Contrast in one study only: volumes are larger and EF more accurate with contrast, so the two are not directly comparable.
  • Limited windows, a different lab or reader, and TTE vs TEE all reduce comparability.
  • Severity words differ between readers; a one-word shift (trace ↔ mild) is rarely meaningful.
