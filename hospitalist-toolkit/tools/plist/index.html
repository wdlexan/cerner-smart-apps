Daily update from the chart

fills empty cells, rebuilds "check today" · version 22 Nov i
Open your Excel list, press Update: each patient is looked up by the MRN in your file and their chart is read. Only empty cells are filled (age, allergy, gfr, vtep, iv, foley, lab); what you typed is never changed. A check today column is rebuilt each run (new abnormal results, vitals out of range, antibiotic day / IV to PO, electrolytes to recheck, renal dosing, pending tests, Discharge Clinic candidates). Keep the file in your OneDrive folder: in Edge or Chrome the update is saved straight back to it and syncs. Close it in Excel before saving. Read-only: nothing is written to Cerner, and your list never leaves this computer and your OneDrive.
