Patient list from a screenshot → Excel

Take a screenshot of your PowerChart patient list (include the header row: Name, Location, Age …), then paste it here. The picture is read in this browser. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is saved in the toolkit or on GitHub, and the Excel file goes to your computer's Downloads.
Paste a screenshot (Ctrl+V), drag one here, or click to choose a picture
Add more screenshots if the list is longer than the screen. Repeated patients are kept once.
Picture won't read? Paste the list as text instead

Check the list

Yellow = the picture was hard to read there. Check names, rooms and ages against PowerChart. Click any cell to fix it. Discharged patients are left out unless you tick them.
