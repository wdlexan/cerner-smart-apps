Creatinine trend
KDIGO 2012: stage 1 = 1.5-1.9 × baseline (within 7 days) or a rise of 26.5 umol/L or more within 48 h, or urine under 0.5 mL/kg/h for 6-12 h; stage 2 = 2.0-2.9 ×, or under 0.5 mL/kg/h for 12 h or more; stage 3 = 3 × or more, creatinine 354 or more, dialysis started, or under 0.3 mL/kg/h for 24 h or no urine for 12 h.

FENa = (urine Na × serum creatinine) ÷ (serum Na × urine creatinine) × 100; FEUrea the same with urea. FENa is unreliable on diuretics, with CKD, contrast, rhabdomyolysis, early obstruction, GN and hepatorenal syndrome (can be under 1% without being pre-renal). Unverified: interpret with the whole picture.
