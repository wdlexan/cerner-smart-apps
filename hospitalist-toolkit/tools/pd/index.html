PD medications and levodopa-equivalent daily dose (LEDD)
LEDD factors: Jost et al., Movement Disorders 2023. Doses are read from the order text: correct the mg per dose or doses per day if they are wrong.
Doses on time (last 72 h)
PD medications are time-critical: give within 30 minutes of the patient's usual times, including overnight and peri-procedure. Late or missed doses cause rigidity, falls, aspiration and delirium.
