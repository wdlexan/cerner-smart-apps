Pre-op readiness
Acceptable reasons to delay (AAGBI 2011): Hgb below 80; Na below 120 or above 150; K below 2.8 or above 6.0; uncontrolled diabetes; uncontrolled heart failure; correctable arrhythmia (rate above 120); chest infection with sepsis; reversible coagulopathy. Waiting for an echo, a bed or a routine test is not one: the target is theatre within 48 h (sooner is better).
Anticoagulants and antiplatelets
NHS GGC hip fracture anticoagulation guideline (2024) for timing under general anaesthesia; ASRA intervals for spinal. Check with anaesthesia: local practice varies.

Bone health and falls
Osteoporosis Canada 2023: after a hip fracture, treat without needing a bone density test; bisphosphonates first line. Zoledronic acid timing after surgery is local practice.
