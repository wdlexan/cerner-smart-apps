Asplenia and hyposplenia: admitted patient, pneumonia antibiotics, vaccines, precautions

Finds splenectomy, sickle cell and other causes of hyposplenia in the chart, checks for OPSI, picks pneumonia antibiotics (severity, allergy, kidney function, meningitis, Pseudomonas / MRSA risk), works out which vaccines are due and when the next doses are, decides daily prophylaxis vs a standby antibiotic, lists other precautions (bites, ticks, malaria, thrombosis), and drafts the handout and note. Read-only.
Decision-support draft. It orders nothing. Rules were written by Claude from the Canadian Immunization Guide and NACI 2024 pneumococcal statement, the BCCDC Immunization Manual, ACIP meningococcal recommendations, the British Society for Haematology asplenia guideline, the IDSA/ATS CAP and IDSA meningitis guidelines, and have not been verified: check with ID, pharmacy and public health before real use. Vaccines in Cerner are often incomplete: check the provincial record (CareConnect) and paste it below. Use made-up or Cerner sandbox patients until Island Health approves real data.
