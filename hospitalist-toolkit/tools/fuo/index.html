Fever of unknown origin: is it FUO, what fits, what to test next

Fever curve and FUO type (classic, nosocomial, neutropenic, HIV), lab and drug clues from the chart, differential ranked by this patient's clues, staged workup with what is done, HScore (HLH), Yamaguchi (Still's) and GCA criteria, when to treat empirically and when to wait, who to involve, handout and note. Read-only.
Decision-support draft. It orders nothing. Rules were written by Claude from Durack and Street's FUO categories, the Haidar and Singh NEJM 2022 review, Bleeker-Rovers' structured approach, PET-CT studies, the HScore, Yamaguchi and 2022 ACR/EULAR GCA criteria, and have not been verified: check with infectious diseases, rheumatology and hematology before real use. Tests and drugs are found by code and name, so something not shown may still be in the chart (use "Mark" in the workup lists).
