Fluid calculator (Adrogue-Madias estimate)
Change = (infusate Na + K − serum Na) ÷ (total body water + 1) per litre. Ignores urine losses, which often make the real change much larger (especially once ADH switches off). Recheck sodium; do not rely on this.
