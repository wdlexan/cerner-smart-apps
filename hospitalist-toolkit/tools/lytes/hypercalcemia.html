Symptoms and signs

  • Thirst, polyuria (nephrogenic DI), dehydration, AKI, stones
  • Nausea, vomiting, constipation, pancreatitis, peptic ulcer
  • Fatigue, weakness, confusion, depression, drowsiness, coma
  • ECG: short QT, bradycardia, AV block; arrhythmia when severe

Do not

  • Give furosemide to lower calcium: only for fluid overload after volume is restored SfE
  • Continue calcitonin past 48–72 h ES 2023
  • Give zoledronic acid with eGFR < 30 without discussion (dose reduce / avoid; slower infusion)
  • Forget vitamin D: replete deficiency before or after antiresorptives to avoid low Ca

Drugs to stop or review

  • Thiazides (hydrochlorothiazide, chlorthalidone, indapamide)
  • Calcium supplements, calcium-containing antacids
  • Vitamin D, calcitriol, alfacalcidol
  • Lithium (discuss with psychiatry)
  • Teriparatide, abaloparatide; vitamin A / retinoids
  • Recently stopped denosumab: rebound hypercalcemia can follow

Antiresorptive notes

  • Zoledronic acid 4 mg IV over ≥ 15 min; pamidronate 60–90 mg IV over 2–4 h (30–90 mg by severity, at ≤ 20 mg/h SfE). Slow the infusion and reduce the dose with low eGFR SfE
  • Denosumab 120 mg SC: no renal adjustment, more hypocalcemia; suggested over a bisphosphonate for cancer-related hypercalcemia ES 2023
  • Severe cancer-related hypercalcemia: calcitonin plus a bisphosphonate or denosumab first ES 2023
  • Acute-phase reaction (fever, myalgia) after a first bisphosphonate dose is common

Worked example

Total Ca 3.40, albumin 22 g/L → corrected 3.40 + 0.02 × 18 = 3.76 (severe); confused; squamous cell lung cancer; on hydrochlorothiazide and calcium carbonate; eGFR 35.
  • Stop hydrochlorothiazide and calcium carbonate. Saline 1 L/h for 2–3 h, then 200–300 mL/h with fluid balance every shift.
  • Calcitonin 4 units/kg (≈ 280 units for 70 kg) q12h for up to 48–72 h + denosumab 120 mg SC (or zoledronic acid 4 mg if eGFR allows).
  • Ca, creatinine, K at 6 h, then every 6–12 h; Mg and PO4 daily; Ca on day 2–4 for the nadir. Albumin is low: ionized calcium is better.
  • PTH (expect suppressed), PTHrP; goals-of-care conversation (hypercalcemia of malignancy usually means advanced disease).
