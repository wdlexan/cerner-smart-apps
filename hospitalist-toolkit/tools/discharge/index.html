Patient and admission
Open from Cerner to read the chart, or pick a made-up patient:
1. Medicine changes
Compared: home medicines (MedicationStatement / community orders) with the discharge prescriptions if there are any, otherwise the current inpatient orders. "Stop (hospital only)" lines are left off both outputs. Set the status and add a reason: the reason goes to the family doctor and the patient.
2. What changed this admission
3. Pending results
Results not final, orders with no result, and notes saying "pending". Name who follows up each one: under the CPSBC standard the ordering physician stays responsible unless it is handed over.
4. Incidental findings
Sentences in imaging reports and notes with words like nodule, mass, cyst, aneurysm or "recommend follow-up". Untick anything already dealt with.
5. Follow-up in the community
Plans found in the notes read (and imported PDFs or typed notes): referrals to specialists and services, outpatient imaging and procedures, lab tests. Set who arranges each one; they go in the note under "Follow-up in the community".
6. Home care and when to get help
Short plan here, full handout separately. These are the key actions and return precautions for each condition, built into the patient instructions below. For heart failure, also print the full handout with the daily weight log from the Heart failure tab.
