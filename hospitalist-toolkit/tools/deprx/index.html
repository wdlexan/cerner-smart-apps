No patient loaded.
About the ACB score
  • Each drug scores 1 (possible), 2 or 3 (definite anticholinergic effect). Add them up.
  • A total of 3 or more is linked to more cognitive impairment, delirium, falls and higher mortality (each extra point adds risk).
  • Other scales (Drug Burden Index, ARS, Beers Table 7) rank some drugs differently; this tab uses the ACB 2012 update. Transcribed from memory: UNVERIFIED.

STOPPFall drug classes

Sedative hypnotics (benzodiazepines, Z-drugs), antipsychotics, opioids, antidepressants, antiepileptics, diuretics, alpha-blockers (for BP and for the prostate), centrally acting antihypertensives, vasodilators (nitrates), sedating antihistamines, and anticholinergic bladder drugs. Also consider insulin/sulfonylureas (hypoglycemia) and anything causing low BP.
