SMART apps, sandbox only. No patient data.

- `hospitalist-toolkit/` — all tools in one launch, one tab each.
- `choosing-wisely/` — Choosing Wisely Canada checklist on its own launch (also a tab in the toolkit). Demo: `choosing-wisely/index.html?demo=1`.
- `diagnosis-check/` — prompts to rethink a diagnosis: not improving, can't-miss bundles, does the data fit, unexplained results, and imaging findings that need a cause (16 rows: pneumobilia, absent gallbladder, portal gas, pneumatosis, free air, bladder air, splenic/renal infarct, new effusion, pulmonary infarct, adrenal bleed, psoas/epidural collection, portal/mesenteric vein clot, hydronephrosis, bone lesions, lymph nodes, brain bleed/infarct) checked against the chart, with a Rigler triad / gallstone ileus pattern and hedged report statements to close the loop on (also a toolkit tab). Also a CRAB pattern (myeloma), and section 6 Imaging review: a timeline of every study (including older ones and reports inside notes or imported PDFs) with discrepancy flags (organ seen before but absent now, “old” lesion that earlier imaging called normal, note calls imaging unremarkable when the report lists findings, left/right mismatch, addenda and preliminary reports, imaging ordered with no report, clear chest film with low SpO2). Made-up PDF patients for testing in `hospitalist-toolkit/tests/fixtures/`: `TestPatient_SBO_GallstoneIleus.pdf` (gallstone ileus) and `TestPatient_HipFracture_Lytic.pdf` (pathological hip fracture, myeloma). Demo: `diagnosis-check/index.html?demo=1`.
- `eating-disorder-ed/` — adults with an eating disorder seen often in the ED: past visits, red/amber admission criteria (MEED 2022 / APA 2023), refeeding risk, discharge checklist, care plan placeholder. Standalone launch. Shows whether the patient goes to Internal Medicine or can transfer to the hospitalist service (VGH/RJH pathway). Demo: `eating-disorder-ed/index.html?demo=1` (also 2–5). Also a toolkit tab (Eating disorder (ED), `hospitalist-toolkit/tools/edr/`). Matching made-up chart PDFs for Import PDF in `eating-disorder-ed/test-pdfs/` (5; regenerate with `make-test-pdfs.py`).
- `stroke-followup/` — hospitalist stroke follow-up: tenecteplase/EVT timeline (24 h CT, BP window, when antithrombotics/LMWH/anticoagulation can start), BP target for the first 48 h and long term (CSBPR), LDL target (<1.8 mmol/L) with next lipid step, PT/OT/SLP note summary, posterior-circulation MRI check with HINTS, THRIVE/ASTRAL scores, workup and ESUS tracker, discharge handoff. Demo: `stroke-followup/index.html?demo=1` (also 2, 3). Also a toolkit tab (Stroke follow-up, `hospitalist-toolkit/tools/stroke/`). Matching made-up chart PDFs for Import PDF in `stroke-followup/test-pdfs/` (3; regenerate with `make-test-pdfs.py`).

- `g6pd/` — G6PD Check: active medications compared with CPIC 2023 G6PD risk (high/medium/low/no recommendation, plus methemoglobinemia-risk drugs), G6PD status from labs/problem list/allergies, recognizing acute hemolysis with latest hemolysis labs, a drug lookup, and the G6PD reference sheet with a section dropdown. ## Shared interactive add-on (`interact.js`)
One plain-JavaScript file copied into each app that uses it (now: autoimmune encephalitis, stroke, dementia, hyponatremia and liver; hyponatremia and liver load it through a small `extras.js` so their `app.js` is unchanged). Gives: click a chart match to open the source note with the words highlighted; ✓/✗ on auto-ticks to measure rule accuracy (kept in the browser, CSV export); live countdowns with optional pop-up alerts; trend charts with hover, click and zoom; printable patient/family handouts; values typed once (weight, height) shared by every tab. To add it to another app: copy the file in, load it before the app's script, then call its functions (see the comment at the top of the file).
