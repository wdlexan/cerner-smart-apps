SMART apps, sandbox only. No patient data.

- `hospitalist-toolkit/` — all tools in one launch, one tab each.
- `choosing-wisely/` — Choosing Wisely Canada checklist on its own launch (also a tab in the toolkit). Demo: `choosing-wisely/index.html?demo=1`.
- `diagnosis-check/` — prompts to rethink a diagnosis: not improving, can't-miss bundles, does the data fit, unexplained results (also a toolkit tab). Demo: `diagnosis-check/index.html?demo=1`.
- `eating-disorder-ed/` — adults with an eating disorder seen often in the ED: past visits, red/amber admission criteria (MEED 2022 / APA 2023), refeeding risk, discharge checklist, care plan placeholder. Standalone launch. Shows whether the patient goes to Internal Medicine or can transfer to the hospitalist service (VGH/RJH pathway). Demo: `eating-disorder-ed/index.html?demo=1` (also 2–5).
