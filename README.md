SMART apps, sandbox only. No patient data.

- `hospitalist-toolkit/` — all tools in one launch, one tab each.
- `choosing-wisely/` — Choosing Wisely Canada checklist on its own launch (also a tab in the toolkit). Demo: `choosing-wisely/index.html?demo=1`.
- `diagnosis-check/` — prompts to rethink a diagnosis: not improving, can't-miss bundles, does the data fit, unexplained results, and imaging findings that need a cause (16 rows: pneumobilia, absent gallbladder, portal gas, pneumatosis, free air, bladder air, splenic/renal infarct, new effusion, pulmonary infarct, adrenal bleed, psoas/epidural collection, portal/mesenteric vein clot, hydronephrosis, bone lesions, lymph nodes, brain bleed/infarct) checked against the chart, with a Rigler triad / gallstone ileus pattern and hedged report statements to close the loop on (also a toolkit tab). Made-up PDF patient for testing: `hospitalist-toolkit/tests/fixtures/TestPatient_SBO_GallstoneIleus.pdf`. Demo: `diagnosis-check/index.html?demo=1`.
- `eating-disorder-ed/` — adults with an eating disorder seen often in the ED: past visits, red/amber admission criteria (MEED 2022 / APA 2023), refeeding risk, discharge checklist, care plan placeholder. Standalone launch. Shows whether the patient goes to Internal Medicine or can transfer to the hospitalist service (VGH/RJH pathway). Demo: `eating-disorder-ed/index.html?demo=1` (also 2–5).
- `stroke-followup/` — hospitalist stroke follow-up: tenecteplase/EVT timeline (24 h CT, BP window, when antithrombotics/LMWH/anticoagulation can start), posterior-circulation MRI check with HINTS, THRIVE/ASTRAL scores, workup and ESUS tracker, discharge handoff. Demo: `stroke-followup/index.html?demo=1` (also 2, 3).
- `diabetes-inpatient/` — inpatient diabetes (Type 1 and Type 2 views): 3-day glucose/insulin grid, lows with likely causes, dose suggestions with calculations, non-insulin options for this patient, A1C-guided discharge plan, vascular protection (statin, ACEi/ARB, ASA, SGLT2i/GLP-1 RA). Demo: `diabetes-inpatient/index.html?demo=1` (also 2, 3).
- `watch-ahead/` — what to watch, what may happen, contingency plan, plus a **Second look** section: pick your role (MRP / consulted / co-managing) and the other service; it shows role-specific cognitive-bias prompts, unexplained results and drugs without a listed indication, a role checklist, and scripts for raising a concern with the other service (SBAR, CUS, escalation ladder). Also a toolkit tab (Watch-ahead). Logic in `secondlook.js`, tests in `hospitalist-toolkit/tests/test_secondlook.js`.
